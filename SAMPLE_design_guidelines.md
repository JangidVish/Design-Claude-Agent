## Brand Identity
- **Brand Name:** Nova Digital Agency
- **Industry/Niche:** Digital Marketing & Design Agency
- **Brand Voice & Tone:** Professional yet approachable. Confident and results-driven. Uses minimal emojis. Direct, clear language.
- **Target Audience:** Small to medium business owners, startups, entrepreneurs aged 25–45
- **Tagline:** "Elevate Your Digital Presence"

---

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors:** #1A1A2E (deep navy), #E94560 (vibrant red-pink accent)
- **Secondary Colors:** #F5F5F5 (off-white), #0F3460 (dark blue), #FFFFFF (pure white)
- **Typography Style:** Clean, modern sans-serif. Bold headings, light body text.
- **Logo Style:** Minimalist wordmark with a small geometric icon
- **Overall Aesthetic:** Dark, premium, modern. High contrast. Corporate but creative.

## Design Preferences (for DALL-E prompts)

### Always Include:
- Dark background (deep navy #1A1A2E or near-black)
- Red-pink accent color (#E94560) as a highlight or focal point
- Clean, uncluttered composition with strong visual hierarchy
- Modern, professional, premium brand feel
- High contrast between background and foreground elements
- Geometric or minimal design elements

### Avoid:
- Bright neon colors (except the brand accent red-pink)
- Cluttered or overly busy layouts
- Comic, cartoonish, or hand-drawn styles
- Stock photo clichés (handshakes, people pointing at whiteboards)
- Gradients that clash with the brand palette
- Warm or earthy tones (browns, oranges, yellows)

### Prompt Style Keywords:
`dark navy background, deep blue and vibrant red-pink accent, modern minimalist digital design, clean geometric composition, professional premium aesthetic, high contrast, sharp details, marketing agency style, sleek corporate creative`
